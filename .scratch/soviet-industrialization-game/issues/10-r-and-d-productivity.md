# 10: R&D Funding and Sector Productivity

**What to build:** A fundable R&D system that develops named, sector-specific productivity improvements and applies them through explicit adoption or retrofit.

**Blocked by:** 01: Daily Simulation Slice; 03: Project Lifecycle and Construction Gating

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Recurring R&D funding generates a spendable R&D stockpile.
- [ ] Each productivity improvement has a named sector, research cost, development time, prerequisites, measurable effect, and possible operating or maintenance tradeoff.
- [ ] Future projects can adopt an improvement during design or equipment stages.
- [ ] Existing facilities require retrofit projects with downtime, equipment, labor, and freight costs.
- [ ] Adoption exposes its expected productivity gain, costs, downtime, and new operating requirements before confirmation.
- [ ] R&D effects remain sector-specific rather than becoming an invisible universal technology bonus.

## Answer

Accepted design: R&D funding is a recurring allocation that creates a stockpile. Developing an improvement spends that stockpile; adoption is a separate explicit decision. New projects and existing facilities use different adoption paths so productivity gains cannot be applied retroactively for free.
