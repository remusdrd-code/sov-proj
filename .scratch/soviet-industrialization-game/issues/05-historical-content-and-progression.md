# 05: Resource Flows and Fractional Operation

**What to build:** Commissioned facilities operate at fractional utilization. Fuel, freight, materials, maintenance, services, power, and labor can reduce output and identify a recoverable binding constraint.

**Blocked by:** 03: Project Lifecycle and Construction Gating; 04: Terrain Infrastructure Construction

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A facility with equipment, electricity, and labor can operate after the required construction gates are met.
- [ ] Daily output is fractional and can reach values such as 34% when fuel or freight access is deficient.
- [ ] The system identifies the binding constraint and recovers utilization when that constraint is corrected.
- [ ] Nameplate capacity, commissioned capacity, operating output, and utilization remain distinct in observable state and tests.
